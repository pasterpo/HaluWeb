About HaluWeb

HaluWeb is an ever-growing archive of AI-hallucinated websites. Every time you click Generate, the AI invents a completely fake business, blog, or service from an alternate universe — and it gets saved to the database forever.

Browse thousands of absurd fake websites. Use the Stumble button to discover something random. Vote for your favourites. Share the weirdest ones with your friends.
